## What was tested (in order)

### Round 1: Four plasticity-adjustment directions

Per the grounding-plasticity plan doc, four candidate mechanisms for
making synaptogenesis useful on v0.5. All tested multi-seed
{0, 1, 42} on the 8-regime capacity schedule.

| Direction | Idea | Multi-seed win rate | Verdict |
|-----------|------|---------------------|---------|
| 1: PE-supervised synap | Per-pair EMA of PE∆ on co-activation | 1/8, 1/8, 8/8 | NEGATIVE (seed=42 outlier) |
| 2B: Learnable projections | nn.Parameter proj + prediction loss | 4/8, 4/8, 1/8 | NEGATIVE (regresses on 4x64) |
| 3: Plasticity broadcast | Scalar gain on Hebbian+synap | 5/8, 3/8, 0/8 | NEGATIVE |
| 5: Positional locality | Hard distance cutoff on synap admissions | **8/8, 7/8, 8/8** | **POSITIVE** |

The three failures share a common premise — gate admissions by a
TEMPORAL signal (PE, PE gradient, PE×coactivation). This premise
is wrong on SOMA's substrate because random input projections
destroy any useful temporal correlation structure. Locality
succeeds because it introduces an ORTHOGONAL spatial prior that
the projection noise doesn't corrupt.

### Round 2: Ruling out sparsity as a confound

`synap_only_local` admits 2-3× fewer edges than `synap_only`. Was
the benefit from LOCALITY specifically or just FEWER EDGES?

| Control | Method | Result |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Cap1 | Per-call admission cap = 1 | Matched totals (158), locality still wins 5-8/8 |
| Cap2 | Per-call admission cap = 2 | Matched totals (158), locality still wins 5-8/8 |
| Rate=0.63 | Reduce synap rate from 2.0 to 0.63 | Prob clamps to 1.0, same 158 totals |
| Rate=0.30 | Reduce synap rate to 0.30 | Same 158 totals, locality wins 7-8/8 |
| Cutoff 0.25 | Tighter filter | 0 admissions (too tight), = no_growth |
| Cutoff 0.50 | Reference | SWEET SPOT, 8/8-7/8-7/8 |
| Cutoff 0.75 | Looser filter | 150-158 admissions (too loose), 0-1/8 wins |
| Cutoff 1.00 | Very loose | 150-158 admissions (~synap_only), 0-8/8 mixed |

The cutoff sweep produces a **clean inverted-U**: too tight rejects
all pairs, too loose admits all pairs, sweet spot at 0.5. This is
incompatible with a "sparsity alone" explanation — if fewer edges
were the driver, tighter cutoffs would monotonically help.

**Conclusion**: locality is a real mechanism with a tunable
operating point, not a sparsity artifact.

## Key insight: structural plasticity needs structural priors

Generalizing the v0.5 finding:

> Pattern-based plasticity priors (co-activation, PE correlation)
> are insufficient on substrates where patterns are themselves
> generated by arbitrary upstream randomness. Structural plasticity
> requires STRUCTURAL priors — orthogonal to the projection noise.

Positional locality is one such structural prior. Other candidate
structural priors (not yet tested):
- Semantic similarity from LLM embeddings (Direction 4a)
- Temporal co-occurrence with bounded recency (different from PE-delta)
- Hierarchical clustering on input features

The v0.5 inverted-U suggests these would all need tuning — an
aperture too tight rejects useful edges; too loose admits
arbitrary ones.

## Product implications (honest, calibrated)

**Validated**:
- Locality filter is SAFE to enable (no harm on retrieval benchmarks
  tested; large positive on synthetic prediction).
- 2-3× fewer edges at matched or better performance — modest VRAM /
  latency gains.

**Likely**:
- For SOMA-as-memory-layer product: locality prevents graph-rerank
  catastrophic failure modes at high alpha. Useful for agents
  willing to weight SOMA signals heavily.

**Not validated yet**:
- Locality improving peak retrieval accuracy on real workloads.
  Initial synthetic showed only 1/3 seeds benefit.
- Locality mattering for continual-learning / long-running
  deployments (the scenario where plasticity is most active).

**Still open**:
- Does the v0.5 result generalize to different position_dim /
  jitter / graph size parameterizations? (One cutoff sweep only.)
- Does the benefit hold on text / multimodal substrates where
  positions carry DIFFERENT semantics?
- Position-scramble: do the SPECIFIC positions matter, or any
  coherent distance metric?
